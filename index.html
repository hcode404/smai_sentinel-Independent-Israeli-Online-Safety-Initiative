SMAI Sentinel — Independent Israeli Online Safety Initiative

SMAI Sentinel is an Independent Israeli Online Safety Initiative helping children, teens and parents respond to online harassment, scams, hacked or stolen accounts, impersonation, threats, harmful content and other online risks.

SMAI Sentinel is not Microsoft Sentinel, not SiMa.ai Sentinel, and not a SiMa.ai Modalix DevKit metrics or monitoring tool. SMAI Sentinel is not a government body, law enforcement agency, emergency service or registered nonprofit.

Official website: https://smai-support.jo3.org/. Support/reporting: smai-support@proton.me. Contact: minipro.7548@gmail.com.
