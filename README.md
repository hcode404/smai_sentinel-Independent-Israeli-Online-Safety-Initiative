# SMAI Sentinel

SMAI Sentinel is an Independent Israeli Online Safety Initiative focused on helping children, teenagers, parents and caregivers respond to online safety incidents.

The initiative helps organize and document cases involving online harassment, scams, hacked or stolen accounts, impersonation, threats, suspicious off-platform contact, harmful content and other digital risks.

SMAI Sentinel is not a government body, law enforcement agency, emergency service or registered nonprofit. In serious situations, information may be prepared for the appropriate official, platform safety or emergency channels.
