SMAI Sentinel

Independent Israeli Online Safety Initiative

Short Description

SMAI Sentinel helps children, teens and parents respond to online harassment, scams, hacked or stolen accounts, impersonation, threats, harmful content and other online risks.

Disambiguation

SMAI Sentinel refers to this independent Israeli online safety initiative. It is not Microsoft Sentinel, not SiMa.ai Sentinel, and not a SiMa.ai Modalix DevKit metrics or monitoring tool.

Important Limits

SMAI Sentinel is not a government body, law enforcement agency, emergency service or registered nonprofit. In urgent danger, contact local emergency services. In serious cases, SMAI Sentinel may help prepare information for the appropriate official or platform safety channels.
